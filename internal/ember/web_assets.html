Capability boundary

What Ember does not provide

Kafka

No broker, partitions, consumer groups, offsets, replication, or Kafka protocol adapter exists.

Cloud provider resources

These controls operate Ember's local-first stores and provider contracts only; they do not provision AWS, Azure, or GCP resources.

Secrets / Key Vault

No secret-management provider or credential storage UI exists. The portal never accepts or displays secrets.
